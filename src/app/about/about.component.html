About Ars Humana

I have nothing to say and I am saying it and that is poetry.
वक्तव्यं नास्ति मम — तद् एव वदामि — एषा कविता।
Vaktavyaṁ nāsti mama — tad eva vadāmi — eṣā kavitā.
- John Cage

Ars Humana is a creative exploration of reality, combining data visualization, aesthetics, and metaphysics in an unbroken flow.

The aims to provide a platform for users to explore the intricate nature of high concepts, thier relationships different, symbolic representations, and their significance in understanding the nature of reality.

Technically - this project is built using Angular and D3.js to bring these ideas into an interactive digital form.

This project is conceived, designed, and developed by Jeffrey Radcliffe.
